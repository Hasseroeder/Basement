# Damage Calculation

After a pet uses a weapon, it goes through three main steps to calculate the total damage:  

1. calculating base damage 
2. adding random variance 
3. going through various modifiers

All three of these steps are done for every Attack, Heal, or Replenish,  
but for simplicity they will only be referred to as Attacks or Damage in this section.

Each step will be followed by the example math for a level 50 :eagle: Eagle holding a :f: :f_bow:

## Base Damage

The first step for any attack involves only two numbers:

- the [pet's relevant stat](/pets) (usually :str: STR or :mag: MAG for attacking weapons),
- and the weapon's modifier (e.g. 160% or 1.6 for a :f: :f_bow:).

Since the passives :f_str: Strength and :f_mag: Magic are calculated into the base :strmag: STR/MAG stat, these are also accounted for during this step.

`BaseDamage = STR × WeaponModifier`  
`BaseDamage = (50 × 13 + 100) × 1.6`  
`BaseDamage = 1200`

## Random Bonus

After base damage is calculated, a random ±50 damage is added.  
OwO will pick a random number from -50 to +50 and add it, then it will round to the nearest whole number.

For simplicity, we will be assuming that OwO picked the random number 0, so 1200 is still our total damage to this point.

## Modifiers

###### -# This gets very into the weeds, be warned.

There are many modifiers that can add and subtract from the base damage.  
These factors are categorized into four distinct groups:

- Positive Modifiers (eg. :attup: Attack Up)
- Negative Modifiers (eg. :defup: Defense Up)
- :pr: PR / :mr: MR
- Redirect Modifiers (:sg: Safeguard, :tether: Tether, and :leech: Leech)

These are done step by step, and any increase or decrease to damage is based on the total of the previous step.

### Positive Modifiers

Positive Modifiers include :attup:, :attup+:, :attup++:, :crit:, :enrage:, :gslay:, :resonance:, and :sprout:.  
Each of their buffs is based on the total of the Base Damage done.

In our example, the damage will go through :attup:.

`AfterPosModifiers = BaseDamage + (BaseDamage × Modifier)`  
`AfterPosModifiers = 1200 + (1200 × 0.25)`  
`AfterPosModifiers = 1500`

Note that you can add as many `+ (BaseDamage × Modifier)` terms as you need, adding them all onto the Base Damage.

### Negative Modifiers

Negative Modifiers include :taunt:, :defup:, :mort:, :adapt:, and :resonance:.  
These are stacked multiplicatively, with passives always going before buffs, multiplying on top of the previous step (Positive Modifiers).

In our example, the damage will go through :taunt: Taunt and 1 stack of :resonance: Resonance.

`AfterNegModifiers = AfterPosModifiers × (1 - Modifier)`  
`AfterNegModifiers = 1500 × (1 - 0.5) × (1 - 0.1)`  
`AfterNegModifiers = 675`

Note that you can add as many `× (1 - Modifier)` terms as you need, _multiplying_ them with your current result.

### Resistance

Resistance is the most basic of all of the steps,
simply reducing damage from the previous step by the res % shown on `owoteam`.  
It is important to note that res is rounded as shown, with no decimals after the %.

In our example, the damage will go through 60% :pr: PR.

`AfterRes = AfterNegModifiers × (1 - Resistance)`  
`AfterRes = 675 × (1 - 0.6)`  
`AfterRes = 270`

### Redirect Modifier

Finally comes Redirect Modifiers, which send the damage intercepted to something else.  
There are only three Redirect Modifiers, :sg:, :tether:, and :leech:.  
These are stacked multiplicatively, with passives always going before buffs, multiplying on top of the previous step (Resistance).

In our example, the damage will go through :sg: Safeguard and :tether: Tether.

`FinalDamage = AfterRes × (1 - Modifier)`  
`FinalDamage = 270 × (1 - 0.4) × (1 - 0.4)`  
`FinalDamage = 97.2`

Note that you can add as many `× (1 - Modifier)` terms as you need, _multiplying_ them with your current result.
