## :gain: Gain
Gain directly gives you an amount of essence per hour.
It starts with 0 ess/h at level zero, and each additional level gives +25 ess/h.
At the max level of 200, that's 5000 ess/h.  

As already mentioned, besides Efficiency, this is the second main trait of your huntbot.  
It similarly affects the amount of Essence your huntbot gains, and is thus essential for fast leveling.

### The Efficiency vs. Gain debate

These traits are often compared, especially because the intent in upgrading them is the same. 
Which one should you focus? How much should you focus one over the other?  
Generally speaking, keeping them roughly the same level is advised,
but we'll try to look a bit more into the math behind why this is the case.  

#### 1. Return on Investment
###### [Click here for the calculator.](/huntbot/calculator)

In the case that you want optimal essence return ratios of your upgrades,
you should look at your upgrade costs and the essence gained from said upgrades.
Let's continue with Level 100 Efficiency & Gain, with no Radar nor Patreon for simplicity's sake.
Your upgrade costs would be `31882` and `40530` for Efficiency and Gain respectively.  
An Efficiency upgrade would gain +1 pet/h, which is `+16.09 essence/h`--simply the pet worth assuming no Radar/Patreon.  
You could continue with this hourly ROI now, but I feel like the daily ROI is slightly more intuitive. 
So I multiply this by 24 for `+386.1 essence/day`.   
A Gain upgrade would give `+25 essence/h` which is `+600 essence/day`. <br />

Now, to calculate the return ratios:  
- :efficiency: Efficiency: `386.1 / 31882 = 0.0121`  
  ⇒ An Efficiency Upgrade has a return of `1.21%` per day.
- :gain: Gain: `600 / 40530 = 0.0148`  
  ⇒ A Gain Upgrade has a return of `1.48%` per day.

This would mean you have a better return when leveling Gain,
and it would make more sense to invest into Gain rather than Efficiency.
These ratios change somewhat with every level, and also upon other factors.
In the default scenario, best ROI is achieved by keeping your Gain roughly 10% above your Efficiency.
But if you have Patreon, it more-or-less makes sense to keep them at the same level.

Exactly this calculation is what most HB guides are based on--it's super important to level your huntbot fast, 
as that will also allow you to better invest into your other traits.

#### 2. Other factors to consider
- An Efficiency upgrade will make your HB less efficient.
  Both in Essence/owo and in Exp/owo.
  Furthermore, your efficiency ROI is hampered when you're forced to sell your pets.
  So if you're low on Cash, you might want to hold off on Efficiency upgrades.
- Efficiency is inconsistent, gain is consistent.
- Focusing on efficiency gives more pets along the way.
