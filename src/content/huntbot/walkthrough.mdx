# Walkthrough
I do acknowledge all of this has been extremely theoretical,  
so I'll try to give a rough walkthrough which you can adjust according to your priorities.

Start by investing equally into Efficiency/Gain/Duration. (maybe Gain slightly higher than Efficiency)  
Keep Exp roughly 50% to 75% of the level of these three.  
Upgrade Cost every 20 levels & Radar every 10 levels.  
Through this, first thing that will happen is Cost getting maxed, roughly when Efficiency & Gain are level 90-100.  
Continue doing this until Duration is at 115 (12h). Any more won't really help, so stop upgrading Duration for now.  
Continue until you max Efficiency & Gain.  
Your Radar should be around ~20 and your Exp is still roughly 50% to 75% of your maxed traits.  
Now it's mostly personal preference; do you focus on Exp or Duration? Regardless, those two are next.  
Once they're finished, pump it all into Radar.
