# Walkthrough
I do acknowledge all of this has been extremely theoretical,  
so I'll try to give a rough walkthrough which you can adjust according to your priorities.

1. Start by investing equally into Efficiency, Gain, and Duration.
    * You might want to keep Gain higher than Efficiency. The [Huntbot Calculator](/huntbot/calculator) helps with the ratio.
    * Keep Exp roughly 50% to 75% of the level of these three.
    * Upgrade Cost every 20 levels and Radar every 10 levels.
2. Cost will be maxed by the time Efficiency and Gain reach level 90-100.
    * Continue with the rest as in the previous step.
3. Duration reaches 115 Level (12h) -- stop upgrading it for now.
    * Continue with the rest as in the previous step.
4. The next maxed traits will be Efficiency and Gain.
    * Exp is still 50% to 75% of your just maxed traits.
    * Duration is still at level ~115
    * Radar at level ~20
    * Choose Exp or Duration to be your next focus. The choice is mostly personal preference.
6. Once you are finished with your preferred trait, max the other.
7. Now, all that's left is Radar.
