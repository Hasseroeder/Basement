## :experience: Experience ( alias `xp` )

You start with 0 Exp/h and gain +35 Exp/h per upgrade. At the maximum level of 200, you gain 7000 Exp/hour.  
This is actually a huge amount per day, and for many people the main source of Exp. 
You probably shouldn't invest too much as a beginner though, as your pets are probably temporary for now anyway.
Investing a bit into the cheap upgrades is a safe bet,
but it probably shouldn't become a focus until after Efficiency & Gain have been maxed.
