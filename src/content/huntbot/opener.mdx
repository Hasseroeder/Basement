# :robot: Huntbot

Former huntbot guides have always tried to give exact instructions, such as "upgrade this twice, then that once".  
I feel that vastly oversimplifies the whole topic though,
so I'll also try to give more insights into how exactly people got to those exact instructions.  
And then you can maybe adjust the instructions slightly based on your own situation!

Upgrade cost grows polynomially, this means that as your huntbot is leveled higher, and becomes more useful, it becomes increasingly expensive to upgrade.  
These are the relevant formulas for upgrading to level x from the previous level:  

`Efficiency(x)  = floor(10x^1.748) for x ≤ 215`  
`Gain(x)        = floor(10x^1.800) for x ≤ 200`  
`Duration(x)    = floor(10x^1.700) for x ≤ 235`  
`Exp(x)         = floor(10x^1.800) for x ≤ 200`  
`Cost(x)        = floor(1000x^3.4) for x ≤ 5  `  
`Radar(x)       = floor(50x^2.500) for x ≤ 999`
