## :duration: Duration ( alias `time` )

Your huntbot starts with a duration of 30 minutes. And every Duration level adds +6 minutes (0.1 hours) onto that.
At the max level of 235, you have a full day.  
Duration is just a convenience upgrade.
If you're very attentive to your huntbot, then all levels of duration are practically the same.
But I'm lazy, and you probably are too.
So invest into Duration to the point that you can have your huntbot running for the entire day with little effort.
This is probably ~8h for most people, as that's how long they sleep, and it also nicely divides the day into 3 huntbots.
But I'm *super* lazy and focused Duration until I had 12h cycles, which gave me 2 huntbots per day. :colon_three:

This concept of cycles is pretty important; 
Any duration longer than 12h won't help you--until you reach 24h--you will still have 2 huntbots per day.
Once you have the primary two traits maxed, then you can think about further leveling Duration & maxing it.

`owo hb 12h` using this time format is the fastest way of sending your hb out with a given time.
I used to specify cowoncy for way too long, because I didn't know of this format.
