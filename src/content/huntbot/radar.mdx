## :radar: radar

:bot: Bot chance is 0% by default, and every Radar upgrade adds +0.000004% onto that.  
The max level is technically 999, but this can't be achieved, which is why there's no real "maxed" huntbot.  
Whenever we refer to such, we only mean a huntbot that has had everything but Radar maxed.

Because :bot: Bots pets sacrifice for more than :common: Common pets,
we could also talk about the *Return on Investment* here, such as with Gain & Efficiency. 
It doesn't make much sense to do this, as the effects are miniscule,
but I've included the calculation on the calculator anyway.
Going solely by ROI, you should have a level 2 Radar by the time Efficiency & Gain maxed.

Though, that's probably not why you're considering Radar.  
But even as the biggest Bot-enthusiast, you shouldn't have Radar over level 30 by the time you're maxed.  
It's just way too expensive and probably has no payoff.  

#### math 😱

We'll calculate some more; this time Bot chances.  
Assuming you have a level 20 Radar, your base chance is **0.00008%**.  
Thus, our chance of not getting a bot for each individual pet is: `NoBot = 100% - 0.00008%`  

Imagine we have a maxed huntbot and we leave it running for 5 weeks.  
We will have gotten `35 * 5760 = 201600` pets by then.  

Now if we take `Unlucky = NoBot ^ pets`, then we'll have the chance of not having gotten a Bot within so many pets.  

Now your chances of getting a :bot: Bot pet are `100% - Unlucky`  
In this example, within 5 weeks we had a Bot chance of `0.14895`, i.e. **14.895%**!
