`  500 + 2 * Lvl * Stat = HP       `
`  100 +     Lvl * Stat = STR      `
`  25  + 2 * Lvl * Stat = iPR      `

`  500 + 2 * Lvl * Stat = WP       `
`  100 +     Lvl * Stat = MAG      `
`  25  + 2 * Lvl * Stat = iMR      `

Hence, a level 20 fox would have:

`  500 + 2 * 20  * 4    = 660 HP   `
`  100 +     20  * 9    = 280 STR  `
`  25  + 2 * 20  * 1    = 65  iPR  `

`  500 + 2 * 20  * 3    = 620 WP   `
`  100 +     20  * 1    = 120 MAG  `
`  25  + 2 * 20  * 2    = 105 iMR  `
