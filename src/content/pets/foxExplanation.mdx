We can see Fox with base stats of:
- :hp: 4 Health
- :str: 9 Strength
- :pr: 1 Physical Resistance
- :wp: 3 Weapon Points (basically Mana)
- :mag: 1 Magic
- :mr: 2 Magical resistance

These stats represent a tanky strength-based attacker, with a bit of WP.  
⇒ I also see 20 stats overall, Fox is a **20-Stat**.  
Pet stats are often talked about in this format: `Hp.Str.Pr.Wp.Mag.Mr`.  
Thus, our Fox would be a `4.9.1.3.1.2`.  
For simpler pets--like `8.1.1.8.1.1`--people often leave out the unimportant stats, calling the pet an "8 8".
