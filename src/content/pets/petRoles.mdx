While there are only rough and vague categories of pets.  
I'll still try to give a basic understanding of why we call pets a certain way.

## # Tanks
Tanks are pets based on tons of :hp: Health and :pr::mr: Resistances,
doing as their name would suggest--tanking.
#### ## **Pure Tanks** :lobbot: :giraffbot: 
- Pure Tanks are the most extreme of Tanks, allocating all their stats into :hp: HP, :pr: PR, and :mr: MR.  
- In current meta, they're commonly seen holding critically important weapons while being replenished with :wp: WP.
  
#### ## **WP Tanks** :slothbot: :dinobot: 
- WP Tanks are similar to Pure Tanks, with some added :wp: WP. 
- On teams where you can't count on replenishment,
  choosing a pet with extra :wp: WP is the only way you can ensure reliable procs of your weapons.
  
#### ## **Hybrid Tanks** :gorilla: :snail:
- Hybrids--true to their name--are to some extent both Attackers & Tanks, performing multipurpose roles.
  - A :gorilla: Gorilla with :f_shield: Shield for example, keeps :taunt: taunting the enemy,  
  and thus protects its team, but also attacks with its :str: Strength on off-turns.  
  - A :snail: Snail with :f_sstaff: Sstaff in Pstall on the other hand, can tank quite a bit of damage and survive,  
  while also using its Sstaff effectively.

## # Attackers and Supporters
Attackers and Supporters are pets based mostly on :str: Strength or :mag: Magic,
as these are the stats that their weapons act upon.  
#### ## Attackers :snake: :lizard: :monkey:
- A pet holding a sword for example, deals damage based on it's strength, 
  so it makes sense to maximise this stat as much as possible.
  The same also applies to the magical side of pets too.
- And there's even attackers that have both high strength and magic, such as :monkey: Monkey!
  But these pets are usually considered quite useless,
  as the only weapons that can make use of such stats are :rune: Rune and :lrune: Lrune,
  which also work with normal strength or magical attackers.
#### ## Supporters :flamingo: :octopus: :raccoon:
- An attacker can always count on massive replenishment from :mtap: Mtap, 
  and even if that doesn't work, an attacker not acting is likely not going to end in a lost battle.
  But if your healer suddenly stops acting, that very quickly ends up disastrous.
  So in similar logic to WP tanks, supporters have some added WP to ensure their acting,
  giving up some power in exchange.
  - :mag: Magic-based supporters are commonly called Healers and Replenishers.
  - :str: Strength-based supporters don't have an established name,
    but are commonly seen holding buff and debuff weapons like Banner & Axe,
    and thus called "Banner Holder" or "Axe Holder", depending on the situation.

<br />

Other useful terms:
- **Gems** are pets with near zero, or zero stats in tankiness, and are usually considered the glass cannons of the game.
- **Bots** are pets with zeroed strength and magic, which makes them uniquely suited to tanking.
  I've explained a little more above in *Effective Stats*.
