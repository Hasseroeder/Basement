### Exp Multiplier

Pets on your **primary** team gain a multiplier on their battle Exp when they battle with a higher leveled ally.  
OwO gives the lower leveled pet a multiplier for it to "catch up" to the higher leveled one.  
You could imagine it as your more experienced pet "training" the other.  

The multiplier is equal to: `(2 + .1 × level_difference)`
