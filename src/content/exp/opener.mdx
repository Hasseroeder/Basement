# Pet Exp

Pet experience is gained by a couple of methods, including battling, hunting, and also just by doing absolutely nothing.  
I won't be talking about Profile exp here, since I honestly just find that boring as hell.

You'll see 2 or 3 teams in your `owotm`, which we call "Primary", "Secondary", and "Tertiary",
the last of which is exclusive to uncommon and above Patreons.  
Your Primary team is the selected one, and the others are to the right of this,
cycling back to the start after the third slot.  
Generally speaking, your Primary gains full exp, Secondary gains half exp and :patreon: Tertiary gains no exp.

Exp doesn't stack if you have Pets on both your Primary & Secondary,
so it generally doesn't make much sense to share pets between these teams.  
Stacking Pets or even entire Teams in the Tertiary team is quite common though,
as there's no reason not to, and you're getting extra Blitz performance for free.

The basic exp needed per level up is: `1000 + level⁴`

## Battle Exp :crossed_swords:

Your main team gains 200/100/50 Exp on a Win/Tie/Loss.  
And your secondary team passively gains 100/50/25 Exp for the same.

This also happens when others encounter you in random battles,
and not just when you grind `owob` yourself.  
Your streaks are unaffected by these battles though,
and due to how rarely you're encountered by random people,
the amount of gained exp is miniscule compared to grinding `owob` yourself.  
We typically call this "Blitz" exp,
because it's more noticeable when your blitz team kills some high level player and you gain a bunch of Bonus exp.

### Bonus Exp is gained in two ways:

- By battling teams that have a higher average level than yours.  
  Which is very useful for new players, but quickly becomes a rarity once you've got a leveled team yourself.  
  The amount of this Bonus Exp is calculated as such: `600 × (avgEnemyLevel - avgAllyLevel)`
- By reaching certain streak milestones, which is where the real gains lie. :stonks:  
  Multiples of 10, 50, 100, 500 and 1000 give Bonus Exp:

  |`1000`| ⇀ |`250√x + 12500`|
