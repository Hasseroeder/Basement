## Huntbot Exp :robot:

This ends up being the biggest contribution to leveling pets for the more inactive players.  
At a maximum level of 200, the :crossed_swords: Exp trait provides 7000 Exp per hour. ( 168k /day )  

Personally, for my team and situation, that's as much Exp as ~300 owoh & owob would give.
So that's quite a big amount for most people.
